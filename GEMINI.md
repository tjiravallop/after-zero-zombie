# Shared AI Skills Policy

Shared skills live in `.agents/skills/` and are available to Google Antigravity. Ponytail and Caveman are global; do not reinstall, copy, or modify them.

- Simple tasks: work directly.
- Medium tasks: load only relevant skills.
- Complex features: use brainstorming and planning.
- Important logic: use TDD.
- Difficult bugs: use systematic debugging.
- Significant changes: review and verify.

Preserve existing user changes. Check Git status before major work. Do not commit, push, or destructively roll back without authorization. Do not claim tests passed unless they ran.
